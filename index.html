How I work

What makes a partnership work

Start with what both sides need

A good partnership gives each side something it couldn't get alone. For Coinbase and the Nike Melbourne Marathon, that was an NFT integration I came up with. It drove a 10x increase in new user acquisition.

Know the other side of the table

I've negotiated for properties like the NBA, Formula 1 and HYROX, so I know what a property will give if you push, where the value hides in a contract, and which assets actually move a brand's numbers.

Bring the right people together

Big partnerships rarely rest on one relationship. I've worked with senior teams at WPP, Omnicom, Publicis and Hakuhodo, and as Global Category Lead for Crypto & Blockchain I advised teams in other markets on their propositions.

The signature is the start

What counts is what the partnership delivers. I delivered the BMW International Open end to end, and ran on-site marketing for FIBA's partners at the 2023 Basketball World Cup in Manila.
